Apple Music, native on your GNOME desktop

RingoMusic is a free, open-source Apple Music client for Linux. It looks and works like a GNOME app, because it is one: Rust, GTK 4, and libadwaita.

Download for Linux View source on GitHub

Free. Apple Music subscription required. x86_64 Linux.

Why RingoMusic

RingoMusic brings your Apple Music library to the Linux desktop without a browser tab.

Native GNOME look

RingoMusic uses GTK 4 and libadwaita. It follows your light or dark style and your accent color. The layout adapts to narrow windows down to 360 pixels.

Desktop integration

Control playback with media keys and the GNOME Shell media controls (MPRIS). Every command has a keyboard shortcut.

Privacy

RingoMusic never reads or stores your Apple ID credentials. It has no telemetry and no tracking.

Free software

RingoMusic is written in Rust and licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later. Development is open on GitHub.

Browse, search, and play. Every view of RingoMusic follows the GNOME design guidelines.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about RingoMusic.

What is RingoMusic?

RingoMusic is a free, open-source Apple Music client for Linux. It is a native GNOME app, written in Rust with GTK 4 and libadwaita. Use it to browse your library, search the catalog, and play songs, albums, and playlists.

Is RingoMusic made by Apple?

No. RingoMusic is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Apple Inc. Apple Music is a trademark of Apple Inc.

Do I need an Apple Music subscription?

Yes. RingoMusic plays music from your Apple Music account. You must have an active Apple Music subscription.

Which Linux distributions does RingoMusic support?

RingoMusic supports x86_64 Linux with GTK 4.18, libadwaita 1.7, and glibc 2.41 or newer. The minimum distributions are Debian 13, Ubuntu 26.04 LTS, and the two supported Fedora releases. Older distributions, such as Ubuntu 24.04, are not supported.

Which audio quality does RingoMusic play?

RingoMusic plays AAC at 256 kbps. The web playback path of Apple Music gives this quality only. Lossless and Dolby Atmos audio are not available.

Can I download songs with RingoMusic?

No. RingoMusic does not download, export, or record audio. This rule is permanent.

Does RingoMusic store my Apple ID password?

No. You sign in on the Apple Music web page inside the playback engine. RingoMusic never reads or stores your credentials. RingoMusic has no telemetry.

Does RingoMusic work on ARM (aarch64)?

No. No official Widevine module exists for aarch64 Linux. RingoMusic supports x86_64 only.

Is RingoMusic free?

Yes. RingoMusic is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later. The Apple Music subscription is not free.
